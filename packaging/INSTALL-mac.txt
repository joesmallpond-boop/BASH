BASH! by Kalabash, for Ableton Live (Max for Live)
==================================================

Three devices:
  BASH!            sequencer + synth / slicer / drums, the whole thing
  BASH! Voice      the synth / slicer / drums on their own, played from clips or a keyboard
  BASH! Sequencer  the generative acid + step sequencers as a MIDI effect: put it before
                   any instrument. Target: Notes (melodies in key), Slices (C1 upwards,
                   for Simpler / Sampler in slice mode, "Slices" sets how many) or
                   Drum Rack (pads from C1)

Install
-------
1. Quit Live.
2. Double-click "Install BASH!.pkg". It puts:
     BASH!, BASH! Voice   -> your Live User Library > Presets > Instruments > Max Instrument > Kalabash
     BASH! Sequencer      -> your Live User Library > Presets > MIDI Effects > Max MIDI Effect > Kalabash
     the BASH package     -> Documents/Max 9/Packages and Documents/Max 8/Packages
                             (the bash~ object and the display scripts the device uses)
3. Open Live: Browser > User Library > Presets > ... > Kalabash, drag a device onto a MIDI track.

Needs Live 11 or 12 Suite (or Live Standard with Max for Live). Live 12 is needed
for BASH! to follow the set's key and scale.

The installer isn't notarised yet, so the first time macOS may say it "can't be
opened": right-click (Control-click) the .pkg, choose Open, then Open again.

If your User Library is somewhere else, drag the .amxd files from "Manual install" in
this DMG into it. If Live says it can't find "bash~", copy the "Manual install/BASH"
folder into Documents/Max 9/Packages (and Max 8/Packages), then reload the device.

Using it
--------
- Press play in Live: BASH! follows Live's tempo and position. With Live stopped,
  BASH!'s own PLAY button runs it at Live's tempo.
- Scale on (top left): BASH! follows the set's key and scale like Live's scale-aware
  devices, and stops quantising when Live's Scale is switched off (Live 12).
- Left: the sequencer. GENERATIVE (the acid generator: Type, Density, Steps...) or
  STEP (16 lanes, each with its own length, speed, algorithm and die).
- Right: the voice. SYNTH, SLICER (drop or load a sample, or pick a loop) or DRUMS
  (16 pads: pick a kit, a sound per pad, or drop samples on the selected pad).
  MOD: two LFOs, Life (slow drift) and six modulation slots.
- Every knob is a Live parameter: automate it, map it in a rack, MIDI-map it.
- DICE rolls the sequencer (UNDO brings it back). Notes from a clip or keyboard
  play the voice directly (THRU), or feed the Arpeggiator algorithm.
