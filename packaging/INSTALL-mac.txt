BASH! - an all-in-one kalabash solution
=======================================

Install
-------
Double-click "Install BASH!.pkg" and follow the steps. It installs:

  BASH! app (standalone)   ->  Applications
  BASH! VST3               ->  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3      (Ableton, Bitwig, Cubase, Reaper, FL...)
  BASH! Audio Unit         ->  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (Logic, GarageBand...)

Untick anything you don't want under "Customize".

The installer isn't notarised yet, so the first time macOS may say it
"can't be opened". Right-click (or Control-click) the .pkg and choose Open,
then Open again. Or: System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".

Restart your DAW afterwards. In Logic, BASH! is under AU Instruments > Kalabash.

Using it
--------
- Load BASH! as an instrument. Press play in your DAW: track 1 plays.
- In the BASH! app, press Play (top bar) to run its own clock.
- The die in the top-right corner rolls everything; right-click it for options.
- Drag the WAV / MIDI chips (Sequencer panel, mixer strips, master MIX) into
  your DAW or onto the desktop.
